What We Support

Different problems. One place to start.

You can contact Solution Bridge about a range of online safety concerns. You do not need to know the legal or technical terminology before contacting us.

💬

Cyberbullying

Repeated insults, humiliation, threats, targeting, or harmful behaviour through digital platforms.

🔒

Online Blackmail

Threats to publish private information, images, conversations, or other material unless demands are met.

⚠️

Online Harassment

Unwanted messages, stalking, intimidation, sexual harassment, or persistent abusive communication.

🎭

Impersonation

Fake profiles, identity misuse, or accounts pretending to be you or someone you know.

📱

Threats & Abuse

Online threats or abusive conduct that may create safety concerns in digital or offline life.

🧭

Other Online Harm

If your experience does not fit these categories, you can still contact us and explain what happened.

The Support Process

What happens after you contact us?

A simple process designed to help you understand your options without making promises about outcomes.

1

Tell us what happened

Send a message through email or the contact form. Share only information you are comfortable providing.

2

We communicate with you

We review the information and communicate with you by email to understand the situation and your needs.

3

Discuss possible steps

We can provide general guidance on documentation, reporting options, account safety, and available support.

4

Connect with relevant help

Where appropriate, we can help identify the relevant authority, platform, service, or support organisation to contact.

Before You Contact Us

Protect yourself and your evidence.

These are general safety suggestions. They are not a substitute for emergency or professional legal assistance.

📁 If it is safe to do so

  • ✓Keep screenshots, URLs, usernames, dates, and relevant messages.
  • ✓Save original files rather than editing or cropping the only copy.
  • ✓Review privacy and security settings on affected accounts.
  • ✓Consider reporting abusive content through the platform's official reporting tools.
  • ✓Do not delete useful evidence just because it is upsetting to look at.

🚨 If you are in immediate danger

  • !Contact your local emergency service or law-enforcement authority immediately.
  • !If someone is threatening physical harm, do not rely only on an online support service.
  • !If you are a minor, consider telling a trusted parent, guardian, teacher, or other responsible adult.
  • !For urgent situations, seek help from someone you trust who can be physically present with you.
